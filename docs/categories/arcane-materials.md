# Arcane — Materials & Ingredients

> Crafting reagents, synthetic gems, and magical components.

---

### Basic Materials
- **Ender Essence** (rcane.materials.ender_essence): Compressed ender magic.
- **Potent Spider Web** (rcane.materials.potent_spider_web): Sticky web compound.
- **Hardened Coal I / II / Max** (rcane.materials.hardened_coal_{1,2,max}): Pressurized coal.
- **Hardened Base I / II / Max** (rcane.materials.hardened_base_{1,2,max}): Compressed clay-copper.
- **Synthetic Diamond** (rcane.materials.synthetic_diamond): Artificial diamond.
- **Synthetic Emerald** (rcane.materials.synthetic_emerald): Artificial emerald.

### Magical Components
- **Echoing Core** (rcane.materials.echoing_core): Resonating deep-sea core.
- **Immolation Totem** (rcane.materials.immolation_totem): Volatile fire reagent.
- **Nature's Embrace** (rcane.materials.natures_embrace): Green orb of forest power.
- **Sun's Brilliance** (rcane.materials.suns_brilliance): Solar-infused flower.
- **Empty Vial** (rcane.materials.empty_vial): Glass container for blood.
- **Blood** (rcane.materials.blood): Player blood.
- **Poison Vial** (rcane.materials.poison_vial): Concentrated venom.
- **Soul Orb** (rcane.materials.soul_orb): Drops from mobs, grants XP.
- **Lightning Essence** (rcane.materials.lightning_essence): Rare drop from Creepers struck by lightning.
- **Link Stone** (rcane.materials.link_stone): Crystal binding agent.
- **Transmutation Core** (rcane.materials.transmutation_core): Pulsing matter-changing core.

### Alloys
- **Blue Gold** (rcane.materials.blue_gold): Gold-iron alloy.
- **Rose Gold** (rcane.materials.rose_gold): Copper-gold alloy.
- **Bronzed Steel** (rcane.materials.bronzed_steel): Copper-iron steel.
- **Abyssal Alloy** (rcane.materials.abyssal_alloy): Blood-infused nether alloy.
- **Crushed Ender Dust** (rcane.materials.crushed_ender_dust): Fine ender powder.
- **Fractured Geode** (rcane.materials.fractured_geode): Cracked mineral shell.

### Explorer Materials
- **Core of Heat** (explorer.materials.core_of_heat): Hot orb of compressed magma.
- **Resonant Plate** (explorer.materials.resonant_plate): Elastic force-reflecting plate.
- **Blessing of the Void** (explorer.materials.blessing_of_the_void): Ender-encased elytra.
